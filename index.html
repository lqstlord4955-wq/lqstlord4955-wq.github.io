Solis

Solis is a private, single-user personal assistant. It runs on its owner's own computer and talks only to its owner through Telegram.

With the owner's permission it can read their Gmail and Google Calendar to answer questions such as "any unread email from my professor?" or "am I free tomorrow afternoon?". It cannot send, delete or change email, and it cannot create or change calendar events.

Solis is not offered to the public and has no other users.
