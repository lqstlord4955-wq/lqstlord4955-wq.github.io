Solis privacy policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

Solis is a personal assistant used by a single person (its owner) on their own computer. It is not a public service.

What Google data Solis accesses

  • Gmail, read-only (gmail.readonly): to search and read messages when the owner asks, and to summarise them or draft a reply that the owner copies and sends themselves.
  • Google Calendar events, read-only (calendar.events.readonly): to show events and check free time when the owner asks.

Solis never sends, deletes, archives, labels or modifies email, and never creates, edits or deletes calendar events.

How the data is used and stored

  • Data is fetched only when the owner asks a question, and is shown only to the owner in their private Telegram chat.
  • To answer, the relevant text may be sent to an AI language model provider (OpenRouter) for that single request.
  • The Google access token is stored only on the owner's computer. Email and calendar content is not copied into a separate database, except that the owner's own chat with Solis (which may quote it) is kept locally until the owner clears it.
  • Google data is not sold, shared with third parties for advertising, or used to train AI models.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Solis's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
